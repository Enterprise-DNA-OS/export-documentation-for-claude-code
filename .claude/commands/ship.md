description: Record that the goods are on board, with the bill of lading or air waybill.
---

Run `npm run trade -- ship <shipment> --on=YYYY-MM-DD --bl=<number>`. It refuses when the export entry or a health certificate or permit is missing; only override (--override-by) if the operator names who accepts it. Then offer /send and /draft-shipping-advice, or /present for a credit.
