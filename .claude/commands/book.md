---
description: Book an order onto a sailing or flight, with its cut-off, and see the documents it needs and the export entry time.
---

Run `npm run trade -- book <order> --mode=sea|air --cutoff=YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM --etd= --eta= [--carrier=] [--vessel=] [--booking=] [--pol=] [--pod=] [--container=]`. A booking after a credit's latest shipment date is refused unless the operator names who accepts it (--override-by), which is noted on the shipment.
