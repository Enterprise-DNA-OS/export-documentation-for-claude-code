---
description: Enter an export order for a customer from product codes, quantities and prices.
---

Confirm the customer, the lines (CODE:qty:price, price per unit in the order currency), incoterm, currency and exchange rate, any agreement claimed and any letter of credit. Run `npm run trade -- new-order <customer> --lines=... [--po=] [--incoterm=] [--currency=] [--fx=] [--freight=] [--insurance=] [--claim=] [--lc=]`. A claim must be in force with the customer's market.
