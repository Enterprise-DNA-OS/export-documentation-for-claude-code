---
description: Record the origin evidence for a product under an agreement: the criterion, the basis, who decided, and how long it holds.
---

Ask for the agreement, the criterion (WO wholly obtained, PE from originating materials, PSR the product-specific rule, NO not originating), the basis and the evidence held, and an end date if the supplier declarations run out. Run `npm run trade -- origin <product> --agreement= --criterion= --basis="..." --by= [--to=YYYY-MM-DD]`.
